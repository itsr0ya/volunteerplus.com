  • Community Assistant

Location:

Multilink Community Services, 38 Blackwood Road, Logan Central (4114)

Category:

Indoor

Time Required:

Flexible weekday shifts between Monday and Friday, 9:00 am – 3:00 pm
Minimum 3 hours per week | Ideal commitment: 1–2 days per week

About the Role:

Join the friendly team at Multilink Community Services to support daily operations and community programs. This role involves greeting visitors, assisting with light administrative tasks, and helping coordinate local support activities for seniors, families, and multicultural groups.

Skills and Qualities:

  • Friendly and patient with strong communication skills
  • Organised, reliable, and comfortable in a team setting
  • Basic computer literacy (email, data entry, printing)
  • Respectful and inclusive toward people from diverse backgrounds

Benefits:

  • Be part of a multicultural, welcoming community organisation
  • Gain experience in administration and community service coordination
  • Meet new people and contribute to local wellbeing
  • Training and ongoing support provided

What You’ll Do:

  • Welcome visitors and assist them in finding the right services or staff
  • Help staff prepare materials for workshops or community events
  • Provide light administrative support such as printing or data entry
  • Participate in team meetings and share feedback on community needs

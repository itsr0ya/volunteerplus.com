  • Community Driver

Location:

Multilink Community Services, 38 Blackwood Road, Logan Central (4114)

Category:

Outdoor

Time Required:

Flexible shifts between Monday and Friday, 9:00 am – 3:00 pm
Minimum 3 hours per week | Ideal commitment: 1–2 days per week

About the Role:

Help Multilink Community Services support local residents by assisting with short local trips and safe delivery of essential items. You may be helping take small care packs to nearby homes, delivering printed information to community centres, or supporting clients with safe, respectful transport to appointments.

This role is great for someone who enjoys being out in the community, staying active, and making face-to-face contact with people who appreciate the help.

Skills and Qualities:

  • Calm, patient, and reliable
  • Comfortable driving short local routes around Logan
  • Good communicator and respectful with older adults and culturally diverse residents
  • Able to handle light lifting (under 5 kg)

Benefits:

  • Directly support people in the Logan community
  • Meet new people and build social confidence
  • Flexible daytime hours that fit your routine
  • Training, support, and guidance provided by Multilink staff

What You’ll Do:

  • Drive short local trips in Logan (vehicle provided or reimbursed if using your own, depending on program)
  • Deliver small items such as meal packs, information booklets, or event materials
  • Be a friendly point of contact for clients, especially seniors or new arrivals
  • Record completed deliveries or trips (simple log, training provided)

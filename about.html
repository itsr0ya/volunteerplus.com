About Volunteer+

Volunteer+ is a small, local initiative run by community members who believe in the power of seniors to lead and strengthen Logan.

We know that retirement isn't an ending, it's a time for new adventures, connections and sharing a lifetime of experience. Our goal is to make it easy for you to find roles that match your energy level and interests, helping you stay healthy, engaged, and feel a deep sense of belonging.

Our Mission

Partner with local councils, community centres, and charities to share verified volunteering opportunities tailored for senior adults.

Why Volunteer+

Focus on Accessibility: We prioritise roles with low physical commitment, clear instructions, and accessible local venues like libraries and community centres.

Flexible Commitment: We feature roles that only require a few hours a week, so you can volunteer without disrupting your social life or family time.

Accessibility: We strive to meet WCAG Level A accessibility standards to ensure this website is easy to use for everyone, regardless of visual or mobility needs.

  • Math Tutor

Location:

Logan West Library, 69 Grand Plaza Drive, Browns Plains (4118)

Category:

Indoor

Time Required:

Weekly sessions between Monday and Thursday, 3:00 pm – 6:00 pm
Minimum 2 hours per week | Ideal for retired teachers, university students, or anyone confident in numeracy.

About the Role:

Volunteer Math Tutors provide one-on-one or small group support to local students attending the library’s free Homework Help Program. You’ll help learners understand basic to intermediate math concepts, build study confidence, and develop problem-solving strategies in a calm, encouraging environment.

This role suits patient, positive individuals who enjoy helping young people learn and thrive academically.

Skills and Qualities:

  • Strong understanding of primary or high school math
  • Clear and encouraging communication style
  • Ability to explain concepts in simple, engaging ways
  • Patience and empathy with learners of all abilities
  • Blue Card (working with children check) required — guidance provided

Benefits:

  • Help students build confidence and a love for learning
  • Gain tutoring or mentoring experience in an educational setting
  • Be part of a welcoming team of community volunteers
  • Flexible hours that fit your schedule

What You’ll Do:

  • Assist students with math homework and assignments
  • Guide learners through problem-solving steps and study tips
  • Encourage positive learning attitudes and focus
  • Communicate with library staff about attendance and progress

Why Volunteer?

Volunteering offers many benefits for older adults. It helps lower stress and anxiety by building social connections and creating a sense of calm and belonging. It boosts confidence through meaningful contributions that remind you your skills and experience still matter.

Volunteering also provides purpose and joy, adding structure and fulfillment to everyday life, while gentle activities like walking or helping in the community keep you physically active and healthy.

Hear from our Logan volunteers

David (70 years old): “After I retired, I wanted to do something meaningful. I became a Digital Confidence Coach, helping adults learn simple tech skills like video calls and online forms. Seeing someone smile when they finally connect with family online makes every session worth it.”

Sarah (85 years old): "When I moved to a new suburb, I joined the local community garden. A few hours of light gardening each week turned into a circle of new friends, fresh air, and a shared sense of pride in keeping our area green and welcoming.”

Lucey (59 years old): “I volunteer to deliver library books to older neighbours who can’t get out easily. It’s a small task, but each doorstep chat brightens someone’s day — including mine — and reminds me how strong our community really is.”

Location:

Tygum Park, Waterford West QLD (4133)

Category:

Outdoor

Time Required:

Flexible morning shifts between Monday and Saturday, 7:30am – 11:30am
Minimum 2 hours per shift | Ideal commitment: 1–2 mornings per week

About the Role:

Join the local environmental care team at Tygum Park to help keep this beautiful lakeside space clean, safe, and welcoming for the community. Volunteers collect litter, report hazards, and work together to maintain the natural environment — supporting wildlife and improving public enjoyment of the park.

Skills and Qualities:

  • Enjoy working outdoors and contributing to community wellbeing
  • Physically able to walk moderate distances and lift light items
  • Team-oriented with a strong sense of responsibility
  • Respectful of nature and environmentally aware

Benefits:

  • Help preserve one of Logan’s most scenic outdoor spaces
  • Stay active and connected while volunteering outdoors
  • Make visible contributions to community pride and cleanliness
  • All equipment and safety training provided

What You’ll Do:

  • Collect litter from walking paths, picnic areas, and lake edges
  • Separate recyclable materials and dispose of waste responsibly
  • Report any hazards, damaged equipment, or wildlife concerns
  • Work safely with other volunteers and follow site supervision

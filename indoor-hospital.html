  • Wayfinding Assistant

Location:

Logan Hospital, Armstrong Road, Meadowbrook (4131)

Category:

Outdoor

Time Required:

Flexible shifts between Monday and Friday, 8:00 am – 4:00 pm
Minimum 3 hours per shift | Ideal commitment: 1–2 shifts per week

About the Role:

As a Wayfinding Assistant, you’ll be one of the first friendly faces people see when they arrive at Logan Hospital. You’ll help visitors, patients, and families navigate the hospital by providing directions, information, and reassurance in a calm, welcoming manner.

This role is ideal for compassionate individuals who enjoy helping others and creating a positive experience for those who may feel anxious or overwhelmed in a busy healthcare environment.

Skills and Qualities:

  • Warm, approachable, and patient with excellent communication skills
  • Comfortable speaking with a diverse range of people
  • Good sense of direction and ability to remember key hospital locations
  • Reliable and confident working both independently and in a team

Benefits:

  • Gain experience in a professional healthcare setting
  • Develop interpersonal and communication skills
  • Be part of a supportive volunteer team at Logan Hospital
  • Make a meaningful difference in the community every shift

What You’ll Do:

  • Greet visitors at main entrances and help them locate wards, clinics, or services
  • Escort patients or visitors to their destinations when needed
  • Provide information about hospital facilities such as parking, lifts, and reception areas
  • Report any concerns or requests for assistance to hospital staff

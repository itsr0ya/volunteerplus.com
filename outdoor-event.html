Location:

Beenleigh Town Square, Beenleigh QLD (4207)

Category:

Outdoor

Time Required:

Flexible shifts Monday to Saturday, 8am – 4pm
Minimum 3 hours per shift

About the Role:

Support the delivery of community events at Beenleigh Town Square — a lively outdoor space that hosts markets, music performances, and local celebrations. As an Event Assistant, you’ll help set up equipment, welcome attendees, and provide information to visitors. This role suits friendly, hands-on volunteers who enjoy being outdoors and part of the community atmosphere.

Skills and Qualities:

  • Friendly, approachable manner with good communication skills
  • Reliable and comfortable working in busy public spaces
  • Ability to stand and move around for extended periods
  • Team player with a positive attitude

Benefits:

  • Be part of vibrant community events that bring people together
  • Develop event coordination and public communication skills
  • Flexible hours to suit your availability
  • Reference and certificate of contribution available on request

What You’ll Do:

  • Assist with event set-up and pack-down (tables, chairs, signage, and banners)
  • Greet visitors and direct them to stalls or activity areas
  • Provide information about event schedules and facilities
  • Support crowd flow and maintain a safe, welcoming environment
  • Help collect feedback from attendees after the event

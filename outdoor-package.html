Location:

Kingston Butter Factory, Kingston QLD (4114)

Category:

Outdoor

Time Required:

Flexible shifts between Monday to Friday, 9am – 3pm
Minimum 3 hours per week

About the Role:

Help support local community programs by delivering small care packages, library items, or event materials to nearby Logan neighbourhoods.

This role is perfect for volunteers who enjoy being outdoors, staying active, and meeting new people. You’ll work closely with Multilink Community Services and local partners based at the Kingston Butter Factory precinct, helping ensure that deliveries reach residents and organisations safely and on time.

Skills and Qualities:

  • Friendly, reliable, and community-minded
  • Comfortable driving short local routes (vehicle provided)
  • Good communication and time-management skills
  • Ability to handle light lifting (under 5 kg)

Benefits:

  • Meet people from across the Logan community
  • Contribute to local support programs
  • Flexible hours to suit your lifestyle

What You’ll Do:

  • Collect pre-packed items from the Kingston Butter Factory distribution point
  • Deliver items to local centres, libraries, or residents
  • Keep simple delivery records (training provided)
  • Be the friendly face of community support
